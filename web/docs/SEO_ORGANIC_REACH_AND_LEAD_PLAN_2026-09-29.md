# Organic reach and qualified-lead plan — 29 September 2026

## Decision in one paragraph

Make the existing commercial and career pages the best direct answer to their assigned search intent, and measure *qualified organic enquiries* from each page before publishing another large batch. The live site exposes 185 sitemap URLs and sampled pages are crawlable, but there is no current unfiltered Search Console or connected-call baseline in this workspace. More URLs alone cannot establish indexing or more leads. The 17 new India lead pages need a page-by-page keep, enrich, or merge review because they share a template and some repeat existing intent owners. This is the highest-return work for both search visibility and conversion.

The apparent paradox is possible: **organic qualified calls = search impressions × search-result click rate × on-page contact rate × connected-call rate × qualification rate**. A better page can lift the later rates while search impressions fall. The current data cannot tell which factor changed.

**Phase 0 implementation update (29 September):** the 17 root-level pages now have exact-path analytics classifications; the local Search Console comparison and priority public-indexability checks are implemented. The local homepage H1 also replaces the unsupported salary range with the approved guarantee message. The earlier taxonomy gap and live salary headline in the audit table describe the pre-fix state; deployment and a new live check are still required. No live Search Console/GA4/CRM export or authenticated Google index inspection is available yet, so the reach decline and call change still cannot be attributed to the Sunday release.

## Priority 2 — win banking and finance job-intent searches

The site sells training for one Financial Operations Masterclass. Someone searching `banking jobs` may want live vacancies, while `banking operations jobs for freshers` may want role guidance. Match the content to that difference:

| Query group to test against GSC | First owner to improve | What makes the answer distinctive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `banking courses`, `banking and finance course`, `finance courses` | `/courses/` and its existing banking course owner | One offer, precise module scope, hours, project/assessment evidence, cost and support terms. |
| `investment banking operations course`, `KYC AML course`, `finance operations training` | The corresponding existing `/courses/` module guide | Concrete workflow and role fit, with a clear link back to the single Masterclass. |
| `finance course with placement`, `finance course fees`, `finance course after BCom` | `/placements/`, the approved fee owner, and the graduate guide respectively | Exact current terms and decision help; resolve existing overlapping URLs first. |
| `banking operations jobs for freshers`, `investment banking operations jobs`, `KYC analyst jobs`, `finance operations analyst jobs` | Existing role guides under `/career-guides/` | Dated employer requirement patterns and original work examples. Do not imply a vacancy where none exists. |
| `settlement analyst interview questions`, `bank reconciliation example`, `loan operations process`, `payment dispute process` | Existing resources and published articles | Practice tasks and explained answers connected to the relevant role guide. |
| `finance course in Lucknow`, `online finance course India` | `/locations/lucknow/` and `/india/` | Real physical-location facts versus nationwide live-online access. |

These are **candidate query groups, not measured demand or ranking claims**. A sample of current provider pages, including [NSE Academy's investment-banking-operations course](https://www.nseindia.com/static/learn-investment-banking-operation-professional) and [AIMA's operations programme](https://pgcourses.aima.in/programs/investment-banking-operation-program), makes duration/hours, curriculum, case work, certification, career support, and instructor credibility visible. Centaur's strongest competitive answer should make its own verified version of these decision facts equally easy to inspect; it should not copy competitors' claims.

1. Expand the existing **career guides** before creating more URLs: investment banking operations, finance operations, retail banking, KYC/AML, payments, credit/loan operations, reconciliation, trade settlement, and fund accounting. Each guide should cover actual duties, qualifications seen in dated employer postings, tools, a fictional workflow or work sample, interview questions, role boundaries, and a relevant module/mentor contact path.
2. Build a connected **practical evidence library** around original examples: reconcile a payment break; assemble a fictional KYC case; investigate a failed trade settlement; check a loan file; handle a payment dispute. Link each case to its role guide, module, and next-step page. Review regulated details against current RBI, SEBI, NPCI, or other primary source material as relevant.
3. Give broad head terms one strong pillar each and use precise supporting articles for specific questions. Prioritize observed non-brand GSC opportunities where a page already has impressions but ranks below the main results or has weak CTR. Use third-party volume only as a directional input, not a forecast or publish quota.
4. If Centaur has **genuine, current employer vacancies with permission to publish**, a separate jobs section can list an individual opening, employer, location, requirements, date, expiry, and direct application method. Only those individual, currently open job pages should use `JobPosting` structured data. Never mark training/course or generic career-guide pages as jobs.

## Release cadence and acceptance rules

| When | Work | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Fix analytics classification, verify event delivery and call attribution, capture GSC/index evidence, review the homepage salary claim. | No reach or lead totals stated without matching source, dates, and filters. |
| Week 1–2 | Deepen `/courses/`, `/placements/`, fees/eligibility, duration/syllabus, graduate/BCom, India/Lucknow pages; audit the 17 new pages for overlap. | Approved facts; one query owner; direct answer; useful original detail; contextual CTA; self canonical, indexability, sitemap, and relevant internal links. |
| Weeks 3–6 | Expand the 6–10 strongest role guides and 4–6 original practical cases, using existing URLs where possible. | Named reviewer, current primary references where needed, demonstrated unique intent, mobile usability, measured next-step events. |
| Weeks 7–12 | Refresh winners, merge duplicates after GSC review, add only validated content gaps, and seek relevant earned mentions. | Compare complete 28-day windows and qualified organic leads; inspect index status and query ownership after each release. |

Publish in small, reviewed batches. A new page requires an unanswered query, a distinct intent owner, original evidence or practical value, a route from a relevant hub, and a clear qualified-lead path. Do not pursue an arbitrary page count. Sitemaps and structured data help machines understand real pages; they do not train Google to rank the site.

## Scorecard for the weekly review

Track non-brand GSC impressions and clicks; query and page pairs; CTR and position; Google-indexed priority URLs and exclusion reasons; organic landing sessions; contact clicks; connected calls; qualified calls; appointments; enrollments; and qualified enquiries per 100 organic landing sessions. Split by mobile/desktop, brand/non-brand, commercial/role/informational page, and release cohort. Keep `missing` separate from zero. Prioritize **qualified organic enquiries and enrolled students**, then diagnose the earlier funnel stages that limit them.
