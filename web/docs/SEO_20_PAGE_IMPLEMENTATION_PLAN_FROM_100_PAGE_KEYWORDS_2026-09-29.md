# 20-page SEO implementation plan from the 100-page keyword roadmap

**Prepared:** 29 September 2026  
**Scope:** The first 20 rows of `SEO_100_Page_100_Blog_Impact_Plan_2026-09-27.xlsx`, `100 Page Plan` sheet.  
**Purpose:** Convert the highest-priority keyword rows into a controlled page-improvement release plan. This is not a ranking, traffic, or indexing guarantee.

## Source and planning rule

The workbook's observed volume and impact scores are directional evidence from the supplied sources, not current Google forecasts. The live keyword owner map remains the authority for canonical ownership: one search intent should have one primary page. A proposed URL is therefore not automatically a new URL. Where the current site already answers the intent, expand the existing canonical page and link to it from the correct hub.

The current repository has a 728-keyword strategy with 24 registered keyword owners. The top-20 workbook rows currently map to 14 sitemap URLs; 6 proposed paths are absent because their intent is better handled by existing canonical owners. This plan deliberately prevents duplicate course, city-access, placement, and career pages.

## Release sequence

### Sprint 0 - ownership and measurement

1. Export two completed 28-day Search Console windows for every current canonical owner.
2. Separate branded and non-branded queries, then record impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, landing page and qualified enquiry signals where consent and CRM attribution allow.
3. Confirm the canonical owner for each workbook row. Freeze the six duplicate candidates until the owning page is updated and reviewed.
4. Record current title, meta description, H1, canonical, robots directive, sitemap membership, schema, primary image and inbound-link coverage.

### Sprint 1 - commercial and decision pages

Update rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10 and 14. These pages should establish the offer, programme fit, syllabus, module scope and current terms before career and regional pages send users to them.

Acceptance requirements:

- one clear query-intent owner and a descriptive H1;
- unique title and meta description written from the actual page promise;
- current programme facts approved by the business owner;
- visible links to the relevant module, career guide, FAQ and contact path;
- `Course` or `Article` plus `Organization` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD where applicable.

### Sprint 2 - career and module depth

Update rows 3, 8, 12, 13, 18 and 19. Add original workflow explanations, fictional examples, decision aids and role boundaries. Regulated KYC/AML and payment content requires subject-matter review before release.

Acceptance requirements:

- the answer appears in the opening section and is supported by useful subheadings;
- every changing regulatory or payment claim has a dated authoritative source;
- no salary, placement, certification, employment or outcome promise is added without approved evidence;
- at least one hub link and two contextually relevant peer links are visible;
- image has an accurate alt attribute, explicit dimensions and an optimized format.

### Sprint 3 - consolidation and business-review gates

Review rows 7, 11, 15, 16, 17 and 20. Implement only the existing-owner updates that are supported by Search Console evidence and approved business terms. Do not publish the six missing workbook URLs just to increase the page count.

## On-page and technical acceptance checklist

Every changed page must pass these checks before release:

- self-referencing canonical with the trailing-slash URL used by the route registry;
- `index,follow` unless a documented exception applies;
- server/prerendered visible content with one H1 and logical H2/H3 sections;
- unique title, meta description, Open Graph image and descriptive image alt text;
- relevant JSON-LD with valid URLs, dates and organization identity;
- sitemap inclusion and no orphan status;
- links from the relevant hub and to the next useful action;
- no duplicate page created when an existing canonical satisfies the same intent;
- mobile layout, image dimensions, broken-link and build checks passing.

## Measurement after release

Annotate each deployment with the canonical URL, keyword owner, release date, reviewer and content change. Review at 14 days for crawl/render/indexability issues and at 28-56 days for query and conversion signals. Use page-filtered Search Console data rather than workbook volume to decide whether a page needs further expansion, consolidation or a new URL.

Primary metrics:

- non-branded impressions and clicks by canonical page;
- CTR and average position by query cluster;
- query-to-page ownership and cannibalization;
- hub-to-page and page-to-contact/course clicks;
- qualified enquiries, not only raw form submissions;
- indexing, canonical and sitemap status.

This plan does not claim that search engines will index or rank every page. Production deployment, URL Inspection and Search Console evidence remain required after implementation.
