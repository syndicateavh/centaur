# Published-content source policy

The surviving original production bundle,
`dist/assets/index-Dkrbbp0A.js`, records legacy site wording. It is evidence of
what the site previously said, not current proof that prices, support terms,
outcomes, employer relationships, testimonials, or leadership details remain
accurate or approved. Public claims must be checked against current business
records and consent before they are promoted or expanded.

The business owner has explicitly approved publishing the phrase **100% Job
Guarantee Program**. Every prominent use must clearly state that it is open to
graduates and job switchers and that a finance job is guaranteed after
completing the six-week program. The course page should link to the canonical
placement-terms section rather than repeating a terms disclaimer in every
component. Graduation is the stated entry requirement; the site must not add
a separate eligibility wall.
This approval does not establish or authorize invented covered employers,
roles, locations, salaries, timelines, exclusions, or remedies; those details
must come from confirmed current program details.

The 2026 organic-growth work continues to exclude unsupported salary,
employer, testimonial, and numeric outcome claims. Legacy copy may remain in
internal source data for traceability, but must not be restored as a current
public claim without current evidence. Supporting documents should remain in a
restricted business system, not in this repository.
