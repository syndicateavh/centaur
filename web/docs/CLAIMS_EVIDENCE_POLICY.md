# Public claims evidence policy

Centaur Careers publishes outcome, salary, employer, testimonial, guarantee and leadership claims only after an evidence review.

## Required record

Every proposed public claim must have:

- A stable claim ID and exact approved wording.
- A private evidence reference; do not commit student documents to this repository.
- The applicable cohort or date range.
- The calculation method and denominator for any percentage or total.
- Written permission for student identity, testimonial, employer name or logo use.
- A verification date, review date and responsible owner.
- The exact routes on which the wording is approved.

## Publication rules

- A placement percentage must define who entered the denominator and the measurement period.
- A salary statement must identify the cohort, sample size, currency, period and whether compensation was offered or accepted.
- An employer name or logo needs supporting placement information and publication permission.
- A testimonial needs written consent and must not be rewritten into a stronger claim.
- A guarantee needs complete public terms reviewed by the institute's legal adviser.
- A leadership profile needs the person's approval plus verified role and credential information.
- Self-serving ratings are not added to LocalBusiness structured data.

The public registry in `src/content/verifiedClaims.js` remains empty until records complete this review. Supporting documents belong in a restricted business system, not in the website repository.
