# Phase 3: existing high-intent page refresh — 26 September 2026

The [Phase 2 India keyword map](./INDIA_KEYWORD_MAP_2026-09-26.md) supplies the provisional work order. The Search Console workbook available here is filtered to a branded query and a former homepage URL, so this refresh is based on the site's current page roles, visible content and published programme facts. It is **not** a measured response to a confirmed page-level traffic loss.

| Existing page | Reader question addressed | Substantive change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/courses/` | Is this an investment banking operations course, and how do I join? | Added an early direct answer, named the single six-week Masterclass and its operations module, clarified online India and Lucknow access, linked to the role guide and cohort enquiry, and aligned the title and description. |
| `/india/` | Can I join from outside Lucknow? | Made the course and access answer explicit, simplified the programme bridge, and focused the title and H1 on live online access. |
| `/locations/lucknow/` | What can I study in person in Lucknow? | Added a venue and programme answer with a prompt to confirm the current cohort before travelling. |
| `/career-guides/finance-careers-after-graduation/` | Which finance direction or course fits a BCom graduate? | Added a task-based way to compare roles and a direct answer to the course-selection question. |
| `/career-guides/investment-banking-operations/` | What does operations work look like? | Added a clearly labelled hypothetical trade-record mismatch and an appropriate investigation path. |
| `/career-guides/trade-lifecycle/` | How does a trade move through the lifecycle? | Added a simplified worked example that joins the listed steps into one transaction. |
| `/career-guides/kyc-aml-analyst/` | What does a KYC analyst actually do? | Added an illustrative onboarding-file discrepancy with documentation and escalation boundaries. |
| `/career-guides/choosing-finance-career-course/` | What should I verify before paying? | Added three specific questions about the cohort, costs, certificate and support terms. |
| `/resources/investment-banking-interview-questions/` | How do I answer an operations scenario question? | Clarified the resource's operations scope and added a practice answer for a settlement mismatch. |

The changes keep one Financial Operations Masterclass, with subjects presented as modules. Live online access is the published option outside Lucknow; the only published in-person location is Lucknow. Illustrative workflow examples are labelled and do not claim employer-specific policy, facilities, job outcomes, or first-hand experience. Review dates changed only on pages receiving substantive content edits.

The existing course comparison page already provides a neutral, dated, source-linked framework. No competitor facts were changed in this refresh. Proposed new pages and the five regional guides remain in their Phase 2 work order; their page-specific demand and qualified enquiries have not yet been established. After deployment, compare unfiltered Search Console page and query results and GA4 organic landing pages with confirmed enquiries over two equal completed periods before attributing any change to this release.
