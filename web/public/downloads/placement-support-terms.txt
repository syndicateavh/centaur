Centaur Careers
100% Job Guarantee Program - published summary

PROGRAM SCOPE
Centaur Careers publishes a finance job guarantee for graduates and job switchers who complete the six-week Financial Operations Masterclass. Graduation is the program entry requirement.

PUBLISHED PROGRAM INFORMATION
- The program is available live online across India.
- The published in-person learning option is at Mindsprout Career Hub in Lucknow.
- The published career-support pathway describes interview preparation, role guidance, and suitable-opportunity guidance.

GUARANTEE SCOPE
The public guarantee is for a finance job after program completion. The public summary does not name a particular employer, salary, role, or city. Request the complete, current written terms for your cohort before enrolling; this summary does not replace those terms.

BEFORE ENROLLING
Ask Centaur Careers to confirm the cohort's completion and attendance requirements, assessment expectations, support process and period, fees, schedule, learning mode, exclusions, and refund/cancellation terms in writing.
