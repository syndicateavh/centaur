100% Job Guarantee Program — published placement terms summary
Last reviewed: 18 September 2026

PROGRAM SCOPE
The 100% Job Guarantee Program is published with the six-week Financial Operations Masterclass for graduates and job switchers.

PUBLISHED CONDITIONS
- The learner must complete the six-week Financial Operations Masterclass.
- The published program is open to graduates and job switchers across India.
- The published support pathway includes interview preparation, role guidance, and suitable-opportunity guidance.
- Live online learning is available across India; the published in-person option is in Lucknow.

GUARANTEE SCOPE
The published wording refers to a finance job after program completion. It does not, by itself, promise a particular employer, salary, role, city, vacancy, or hiring decision. Employer decisions depend on the role, learner profile, availability, and the applicable process.

BEFORE ENROLLING
Confirm the applicable cohort, eligibility and completion requirements, attendance and assessment expectations, fees, schedule, learning mode, support window, exclusions, refund terms, and the current written placement terms directly with Centaur Careers.

IMPORTANT
This file is a plain-text summary for download and sharing. The current written program terms provided for the applicable cohort control if they differ from this summary.
