Our institute

About Centaur Careers

A banking and finance training institute focused on practical process knowledge, guided application and responsible career preparation.

Why we exist

Make career preparation more practical

Centaur Careers helps learners understand the processes behind entry-level banking and finance work. The aim is to bridge the gap between academic familiarity and the ability to discuss workflows, controls and workplace scenarios clearly.

Training is available through the Lucknow centre and live online formats. Current schedules, fees and availability should always be confirmed with admissions before enrollment.

Our training method

  1. 1

    Understand

    Build clear foundations and learn the language of the process.

  2. 2

    Apply

    Work through examples, process maps, questions and guided scenarios.

  3. 3

    Communicate

    Present knowledge accurately in assignments and interviews.

  4. 4

    Improve

    Use feedback to identify gaps and strengthen readiness.

Working principles

What guides the learning experience

Process before promises

Explain how banking and finance work is performed and avoid relying on unsupported outcome claims.

Practice with context

Connect terminology to scenarios, controls, documents and the decisions learners may encounter.

Clear communication

Help learners explain what they know in professional conversations and entry-level interviews.

Transparent expectations

Separate training and career support from decisions made independently by employers.
