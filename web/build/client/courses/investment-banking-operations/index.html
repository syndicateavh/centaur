Investment operations pathway

Investment Banking Operations Course

Build a working understanding of the post-trade processes used to record, settle, reconcile and report financial-market transactions.

Guided course pathway Lucknow centre Live online option

Course overview

What this pathway covers

This course is designed for learners who want a practical introduction to investment banking operations. Lessons connect process concepts with examples, exercises and interview preparation without promising a particular employer or employment outcome.

Who this is designed for

  • Graduates and final-year students exploring financial-services operations
  • Early-career professionals moving toward investment operations
  • Learners who want guided practice alongside foundational concepts

Curriculum

Skills and process modules

Modules build from foundations toward applied scenarios and interview communication.

01

Markets and trade lifecycle

Understand market participants and the steps between trade capture, confirmation, settlement and reporting.

02

Settlements and reconciliations

Work through settlement instructions, common breaks, exception tracking and reconciliation controls.

03

Corporate actions

Study dividends, interest payments, stock events and the operational flow around mandatory and voluntary events.

04

Fund accounting foundations

Learn the purpose of NAV calculations, cash and position checks, expense treatment and control review.

05

Operational risk and reporting

Recognise process risks, escalation practices, maker-checker controls and useful operational reporting.

06

Interview readiness

Practise explaining operational concepts, approaching scenarios and presenting relevant project work.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain the investment trade lifecycle in clear operational terms
  • Identify common settlement and reconciliation exceptions
  • Understand the purpose of corporate-action and fund-accounting controls
  • Present process knowledge through structured interview answers

Related role areas

Training can support preparation for entry-level discussions in these areas. Role availability and selection are controlled by employers.

  • Investment operations trainee
  • Reconciliation analyst trainee
  • Fund accounting trainee
  • Corporate actions trainee
