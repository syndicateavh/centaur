Retail Banking Course

Learn how everyday banking products, branch processes, customer service and relationship responsibilities fit together.

Guided course pathway Lucknow centre Live online option

Course overview

What this pathway covers

The retail banking course introduces the processes learners are likely to encounter in customer-facing and branch-support environments. Training focuses on product understanding, responsible communication, documentation and process discipline.

Who this is designed for

  • Graduates and final-year students interested in customer-facing banking
  • Learners preparing for branch and relationship-support responsibilities
  • Early-career professionals seeking structured retail banking foundations

Curriculum

Skills and process modules

Modules build from foundations toward applied scenarios and interview communication.

01

Banking products and customers

Review deposit products, payments, service channels and the needs of different customer segments.

02

Branch operations

Understand account-service workflows, documentation, transaction controls and daily operational routines.

03

Relationship management

Practise needs discovery, responsible product explanation, follow-up and long-term customer service.

04

Loan-processing foundations

Learn the purpose of application checks, document collection, verification and hand-offs in lending workflows.

05

KYC and conduct basics

Study customer identification, record quality, escalation and the importance of fair, compliant conduct.

06

Communication and interviews

Practise customer scenarios, professional communication and role-specific interview questions.

Learning outcomes

  • Describe common retail banking products and service journeys
  • Follow the logic of branch and account-service workflows
  • Communicate product information clearly and responsibly
  • Approach customer and process scenarios in structured interviews

Related role areas

Training can support preparation for entry-level discussions in these areas. Role availability and selection are controlled by employers.

  • Branch operations trainee
  • Customer service executive
  • Relationship support trainee
  • Loan-processing trainee
